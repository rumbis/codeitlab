# Quests

The overview page carries a short path through CodeItLab. Eleven quests: connect
an agent, pick models, give it memory, turn on evidence, write a board task,
finish one, build a deck, render a video, connect an engine, reach your phone,
use your keys safely. Each quest says what
to do in one sentence, why it matters in another, and links to the surface where
it happens. There are no points, no badges and no streak to keep: progress is
derived from what the app can already observe, so using CodeItLab is the
progress.

## Turning a quest into a board task

The next quest has an **Add as board task** button. It creates an ordinary
Pending task on the board whose title is the quest title, whose notes are the
lesson, and whose notes also name the route where the work happens. Nothing is
started automatically: you decide when to press Start on the board, and the task
then opens a normal thread.

This is the bridge between learning and doing: read the lesson, keep it as a
task, and it waits in Pending until you have time.

## Keeping progress honest

Quest progress is derived on the server from the same facts as the status counts.
The **Refresh status** button on the overview reloads the catalog and every panel
that reads those facts - quests, memory, routing, policy, REA, integrations,
credentials and studio - so a quest does not stay "not done" after you finished
it elsewhere in the app.

## Progress is never a guess

The card re-reads its facts every time you open the overview and right after you
add a quest as a board task, so a quest you just finished does not stay unticked.
Nothing is cached for long enough to lie.

## What it is not

- It is not a course, a certificate, or a score.
- It does not track you across machines; quest progress comes from the connected
  environment, and an empty environment simply shows fewer quests done.
- It does not create work behind your back. The button is the only way a quest
  becomes a task.
